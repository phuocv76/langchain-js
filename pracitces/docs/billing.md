# Billing Guide

## How am I billed?
Plans are billed monthly or annually. Annual billing saves 20%. Usage above plan
limits is billed at the end of the cycle at the metered rate shown in Billing.

## How do I update my payment method?
Billing > Payment Methods > Add card. The new card becomes the default for the
next invoice. We support Visa, Mastercard, and Amex.

## How do refunds work?
Annual plans are eligible for a prorated refund within 14 days of purchase.
Monthly plans are non-refundable but can be canceled to stop future charges.

## Why was my card declined?
Common reasons: insufficient funds, expired card, or bank fraud protection.
Update the card in Billing > Payment Methods and retry the invoice.

## Where do I find invoices?
Billing > Invoices lists all past invoices as downloadable PDFs.
