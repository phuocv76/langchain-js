# Acme Cloud - Product FAQ

## How do I reset my password?
Go to Settings > Security > Reset Password. A reset link is emailed to your
registered address and expires after 30 minutes. If it expires, request a new one.

## What are the plan limits?
- Free: 1 project, 5 GB storage, community support.
- Pro: 20 projects, 200 GB storage, email support, 99.9% uptime SLA.
- Enterprise: unlimited projects, custom storage, dedicated support, SSO/SAML.

## How do I invite teammates?
Open Team > Members > Invite, enter their email, and pick a role (Viewer, Editor,
Admin). Invites expire after 7 days.

## Where is my data stored?
Data is stored in the region selected at project creation (US, EU, or APAC).
Region cannot be changed after creation; create a new project to migrate.

## How do I export my data?
Settings > Data > Export creates a downloadable archive (JSON + attachments).
Exports for large projects are emailed when ready.
