# Product Warranty Terms

## Standard Warranty Coverage

| Product category | Warranty period | What's covered |
| ---------------- | --------------- | -------------- |
| Electronics (TV, laptop) | 24 months from purchase | Manufacturing defects, power failures |
| Appliances (washer, dryer, fridge) | 18 months from purchase | Motor, pump, control board defects |
| Small appliances (microwave, blender) | 12 months from purchase | Electrical and mechanical failures |

## Exclusions

- Accidental damage, misuse, or unauthorized repairs
- Normal wear and tear (filters, belts, cosmetic scratches)
- Damage from power surges without a surge protector
- Commercial use unless the product is sold as "commercial grade"

## Claim process

1. Provide proof of purchase (receipt or order number).
2. Describe the failure and purchase date.
3. A technician may require photos or a diagnostic visit.
4. Approved repairs use OEM parts; replacements are equivalent models when repair is not economical.

## Refund thresholds

- Repairs or replacements under **$500** can be auto-approved when warranty terms are clear.
- Amounts **$500 or above**, unclear coverage, or legal disputes require **human approval**.

## Extension policy

- Extended warranties add 12 months and must be purchased within 30 days of the original sale.
- Extended coverage does not cover accidental damage unless the "Accidental Protection" rider was purchased.
