# Billing FAQ

## Refund policy

Refunds are available within 14 days of purchase for annual plans. Monthly plans are non-refundable after the billing cycle starts.

## Invoice requests

Customers can download invoices from **Account > Billing > Invoices**.

## Payment failures

When a card fails, we retry for 3 days and send email notifications before suspending the account.

For billing disputes marked as critical, route to human review before replying.
