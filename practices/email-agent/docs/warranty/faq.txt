Warranty FAQ

Q: My washing machine stopped working after 18 months. Is it still under warranty?
A: Standard appliance warranty is 18 months from purchase. If the failure is on the purchase date anniversary or earlier, coverage applies for manufacturing defects. Day 19 of month 19 is out of warranty unless an extension was purchased.

Q: Can I transfer warranty to a new owner?
A: Yes, one transfer is allowed with proof of purchase and a transfer form within the warranty period.

Q: What if I lost my receipt?
A: We can look up orders by email or phone number used at checkout. Without proof, warranty claims may be denied.

Q: Do you cover shipping for warranty repairs?
A: Yes for large appliances. Small electronics under 10 lb require customer-paid inbound shipping; return shipping is covered after approval.

Q: How long do repairs take?
A: Typical turnaround is 7–14 business days after the unit is received at the service center.

Q: When is human approval required?
A: Refunds or replacements over $500, ambiguous policy matches, repeated claims on the same serial number, or legal threats.
