# Return and Exchange Policy

## Return window

- Unopened items: 30 days from delivery for a full refund.
- Opened items: 14 days if unused and in original packaging.
- Defective items: return anytime during the active warranty period.

## Restocking

- 15% restocking fee on opened electronics over $200 unless the item is defective.
- No restocking fee on appliances with confirmed manufacturing defects.

## Exchanges

- Like-for-like exchanges within 30 days when stock is available.
- Upgrades require paying the price difference.

## Refund timing

- Store credit: immediate upon approval.
- Original payment method: 5–10 business days after the item is received at the warehouse.

## Non-returnable

- Software license keys once activated
- Personal care items (opened)
- Custom-built or engraved products
